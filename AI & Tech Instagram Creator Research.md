# AI & Tech Instagram Creator Research

Oct 5, 2026 · @Talal Ahmed

75 original AI/tech creators were found; 15 qualify as high signal, 10 of them under 100K followers, and 20 are selected for Reel-level research.

## How the list was built

Creators were found from the 100xEngineers seed, its related accounts, 2026 AI/tech creator rankings, programming and Indian-tech lists, and builder/indie-hacker sources (Starter Story, build-in-public founders). Meme, aggregator, repost and AI-character pages were excluded; accounts whose originality could not be confirmed are marked Unclear.

**Verified vs estimated.** No source here is Instagram's own analytics, so every metric is third-party. Follower counts are public profile figures captured by trackers and are the most reliable numbers. Typical Reel views are averages scraped from public play counts on 31 March 2026 by Apify across \~2,500 posts and Reels; treat them as a sample, not a guarantee. Highest Reel views are shown only where a source exposed a specific Reel.

**Ranking.** Score out of 5 = 35% organic reach (avg Reel views / followers at the same snapshot) + 35% relevance to an AI-tools series (my 1-5 judgment from bio and niche) + 10% posting consistency (3 = unknown) + 15% originality + 5% audience size. Accounts with no reach data get a neutral 2.5 for reach and are labelled Watch. Follower count can move a score by at most 0.15 points.

**Signal labels.** HIGH = the 15 profiled in section B. Medium = score 3.6+ with reach data. Low = below that. Watch = promising but no Reel view data found.
