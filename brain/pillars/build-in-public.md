# Pillar: Build in Public

**Format:** Reels (talking head + screen recordings) · **Preset:** `bip-raw` · **Vibe:** raw, energetic, honest.

## Story angles (rotate them)
| Angle | Example | Hook type |
|---|---|---|
| Win | "Got my first paying user." | result_first |
| Failure | "I shipped a bug that emailed everyone twice." | mistake |
| Decision | "Why I'm ripping out my database." | contrarian |
| Metric | "Week 3 numbers: 41 signups, 2 paying." | result_first |
| Behind the scenes | "How I actually plan a feature." | curiosity_gap |
| Lesson | "The thing I wish I'd known before launch." | callout |

## Rules
- **Day N continuity.** Every episode carries a "Day N" badge. The current N lives in `memory/long-term/pillars/build-in-public.md`; the strategist confirms it.
- **Always show the screen/product.** At least one screen-recording or product shot per reel.
- **Honesty over polish.** Failures and real numbers outperform highlight reels.
- One specific event per reel (not "this week I did a lot of stuff").
- CTA: follow the journey ("Day 13 tomorrow.").

## Interview questions (strategist, ≤5)
1. What did you build or break since last time?
2. What was the hard part?
3. Any number or result? (users, time, money, errors, lines of code)
4. What's next?
5. (optional) Do you have a screen recording of it?
