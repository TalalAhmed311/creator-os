# Retention Playbook

## Structure (every reel)
**Hook (0–3 s) → Context in one line → Value, delivered fast → Payoff → Loop or CTA**

| Beat id | Job | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `hook` | Stop the scroll. Promise. | ≤ 3 s |
| `context` | Why this matters / where we are. ONE line. | 2–5 s |
| `value` (can be `value-1`, `value-2`…) | The thing itself, as fast as it can be understood. | bulk |
| `payoff` | Deliver the promise from the hook. | 2–6 s |
| `cta` | One action at most. Short. | ≤ 2 s |

## Length
- Default 20–45 s. AI Explained may run 60–90 s only if every second earns its place.
- **One idea per reel.** Extra ideas → `ideas-backlog` → their own reels.

## Pacing
- **Pattern interrupt every 2–4 s**: cut, punch-in zoom, caption pop, B-roll, graphic, or SFX.
- Remove every breath gap > ~250 ms, filler words (um, uh, like, you know), false starts and repeated lines.
- The cut should feel slightly too fast when you watch it once. That's correct.

## Captions
- Always on (most people watch muted).
- 1–3 words at a time, centre, lower-middle (~60–70% height). Keyword/number highlighted in the accent colour.
- Safe zones: nothing important in the top ~12% or bottom ~20% (platform UI).
- Proper nouns and tech terms spelled right (`brain/glossary.txt`).

## Endings
- Loopable: the last line flows back into the first when possible ("…and that's why I'm starting over." → "Day 12…").
- One CTA maximum: follow for the series / comment a word / save this.
- Never end with "that's it", "thanks for watching", or a long outro.

## Script-writing rules that help retention
- Short sentences. Spoken, not written. Contractions.
- Front-load the interesting word in every sentence.
- Replace "very/really/super + adjective" with a number or a concrete detail.
- Every line must either move the story or deliver value. If neither, cut it.
