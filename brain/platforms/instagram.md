# Instagram playbook (@buildwith.talal)

## Reels
- 9:16, 1080×1920, burned-in captions (most people watch muted). Hook spoken **and** on screen in the first 1–2 s
  (≤ 7 words on screen). The Edit Bay rules in `brain/retention-playbook.md` apply.
- Length: 20–45 s for explainers/tool demos; up to ~60 s only when every beat earns it. Rewatches and full watches matter more than length.
- What gets distributed: watch time / completion, then **shares** (DM sends) and **saves**. Write for "send this to a friend who…".
- One idea per reel. Payoff early, then depth. End on a loop or a reason to rewatch rather than "follow for more".
- CTA options that work in this niche: comment-a-keyword for the resource/link (only if you will actually send it),
  "save this for when you…", or a question that invites a real answer.
- Cover: readable title on the cover frame (grid view), consistent style.

## Carousels (learning material, resource lists)
- 1080×1350 (4:5), up to 20 slides; 6–10 is the sweet spot. Slide 1 = hook with a number and who it's for; last slide = CTA.
- One idea per slide, readable in ~2 s; big type; consistent theme (`brain/carousel-themes/`).
- Carousels earn **saves** — make every slide useful on its own; a "save this" slide works.
- Instagram may re-show a carousel starting at slide 2 to people who skipped it — slide 2 should hook too.

## Caption & hashtags
- First line is a second hook (shows before "more"). Then 2–5 short lines of value or context. Then the CTA.
- 3–5 specific hashtags (topic + niche), no walls of tags. Keywords in the caption help search.

## Cadence & habits
- Consistency beats bursts. Reply to comments in the first hour. Pin the best comment / keyword reply.
- Reuse winners: a reel that over-performs → carousel version → follow-up reel ("part 2").
