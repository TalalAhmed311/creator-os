# X playbook (Curio build in public)

## Posts
- 280 characters per post (free accounts). One idea. Lead with the specific: a number, a result, a decision, a screenshot.
- Build in public that works: progress with numbers ("Curio: 0 → 214 waitlist in 9 days"), decisions and trade-offs,
  failures and fixes, short demo videos/GIFs, before/after screenshots. Real > polished.
- Images, screenshots and short videos lift engagement — attach one when you can (`media`).
- **Links reduce reach** — put the link in a reply (`first_comment`), not in the post.
- 0–2 hashtags (often none).

## Threads
- Post 1 carries the hook **and** the promise ("How I got Curio's first 100 users — 7 things that worked:").
- Every post stands alone and is ≤ 280 chars. 4–8 posts. Last post: recap + soft CTA (follow for the build, try Curio).
- Number them only if order matters.

## Habits
- Post daily; reply to people in your niche (replies grow small accounts faster than posts do).
- Repeat what works: a post that over-performs → thread version → follow-up with the result a week later.
