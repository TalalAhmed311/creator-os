# Platform playbooks

Starting rules per platform for the writer. They are **defaults, not facts**: platforms change their ranking often and
nobody outside them knows the exact rules. Two things outrank this folder:
1. **Insights** from the research harness (`./studio research insight top --platform <p>`) — patterns with evidence.
2. **Your own numbers** from HQ (`./studio hq review`) — what works for *your* audience.
When HQ finds a rule here contradicted by your data (seen ≥3×), it proposes an edit — you approve every change to brain/.
