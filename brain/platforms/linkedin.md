# LinkedIn playbook

## Text posts
- Only ~2 lines (≈ 210 characters) show before "…see more" — **that's the hook**. Make the first line a complete, specific claim or result.
- Short paragraphs (1–2 lines), white space, no walls of text. 900–1,800 characters is a good range; hard limit 3,000.
- Shape that works for builders: hook → the story/context (concrete numbers, what you tried) → the lesson (2–4 points) → a question.
- Personal + useful beats generic advice. Specifics: tools, numbers, screenshots, what failed.
- **Links**: put external links in the first comment (`first_comment`), not the body — posts with links are widely reported to get less reach.
- 3–5 hashtags at most, at the end. Tag people only if they're genuinely involved.

## Document carousels (PDF)
- Educational / framework / resource content: a PDF "document" post. Same rules as IG carousels, 4:5 or 1:1, 6–12 pages.
  The Design Desk renders the PDF (`out/carousel.pdf`).

## Video
- Native vertical or square video with captions; first 3 s hook; 30–90 s.

## Habits
- Reply to every comment in the first 1–2 hours (comments early drive distribution). Comment on others' posts in your niche.
- Post on weekdays; consistency (2–4×/week) over volume.
