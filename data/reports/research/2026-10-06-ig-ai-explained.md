# Research — ig-ai-explained

2026-10-06 · instagram · ai-explained · 5 insights · 15 outlier posts

## Summary
103 recent reels from 11 tracked AI creators (views read from the public reels grid, 2026-10-06), 10 broken down. **What wins is the payoff, not the topic or the format**: the same split-screen template did 6.6M (99x) and 0.54x for kallaway; what differed was "here's my exact workflow" vs news plus hype (insight 18). Release news with nothing for the viewer to do flopped twice (0.06x and 0.54x).
**Make next:** (1) AI Explained episodes that untangle something beginners see in the UI, like the model picker or "MCP vs skill vs plugin vs hook". Use one analogy ladder and one example per option (insight 17; askcatgpt's model explainer did 8.8x). (2) Open with a surprising, quantified fact about ChatGPT or Claude, show the proof on screen by 2 s, then give a fix that takes 30 s (insight 16; up to 88x). (3) Cut 2–3 hook variants per episode, post them as trial reels and keep the winner (insight 19). Same topic, 20–90x apart.
**Resources:** sabrina_ramonov's "N videos to watch instead of brainrot" list reels did 6.1x and 3.1x (insight 20, low confidence, one creator). Test it as a reel plus the matching carousel. Carousel reach data wasn't public, so carousel performance is still unproven here.
End every reel by asking viewers to comment a keyword to get a free asset (insight 21). It needs a ManyChat DM flow that the owner sets up.

## Top insights

### 1. Free giveaway behind a comment keyword ('comment EDIT and I'll send you the free skill/prompt/guide') drives comments far above likes on AI tool reels
cta · instagram · confidence medium · seen 1× · 5 posts · avg outlier 6.52×

**How to apply:** Close every tool/explainer reel with 'Follow + comment [ONE WORD tied to the asset] and I'll send you [the free skill / prompt / PDF / repo]'. Make the asset genuinely free and useful (tenfoldmarc's 'edit' reel: 16.9K comments vs 2.5K likes). Needs a DM automation (ManyChat) the owner sets up himself - nothing auto-posts from here. Caveat: flops use it too; it amplifies a good hook, it doesn't rescue a weak one.

### 2. Post 2-4 hook/packaging variants of the same idea close together (trial-reel style) and let the winner run - same topic varies 20-90x
cadence · instagram · confidence medium · seen 1× · 3 posts · avg outlier 10×

**How to apply:** For each AI Explained idea, cut 2-3 versions that differ ONLY in the first 3 s: (a) stat/claim line + title box, (b) question line, (c) visual-first (UI screenshot). Post them as Instagram trial reels (non-followers only) or spaced across the day within the owner's 1-3/day cadence; double down (carousel, follow-up episode) on whichever beats your median by 3x. Evidence: mavgpt 'Honest' prompt 386K vs 4.2K twin, 'Human' prompt 417K vs 4.5K twin; harpercarrollai posted 9 cuts of one Muse video, one hit 92K vs ~2K for the rest.

### 3. Same news, different payoff: 'a way not many people have tried' + my exact personal workflow shown on screen step by step beats announcing the feature + hype
structure · instagram · confidence medium · seen 1× · 2 posts · avg outlier 10×

**How to apply:** Structure: '[TOOL] just [shipped X]. And there's a way of using it not many people have tried that is insanely valuable for [AUDIENCE].' -> 'I used it to build [concrete workflow]' -> steps 1-2-3 as screen recording (top) + face (bottom) with a title bar '[TOOL] FOR [AUDIENCE]' -> name your method -> 2 more use cases -> 'comment [WORD] for the setup'. Counter-evidence in the same week, same format: kallaway's 'ChatGPT can now edit your videos' (news + 'feels like AGI', no workflow) did 0.54x and adamstewart's 'Did OpenAI kill OpenClaw?' did 0.06x. Never ship a release reel without a 'here is what I did with it'.

- [@kallaway](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd1q-AXuj3O/) — 6.6M views, 98.95× their normal — “Google Gemini just integrated agentic capabilities in Gemini Live, and there's a way of us”
- [@mavgpt](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeEmrbIK36S/) — 67.2K views, 15.32× their normal

### 4. Surprising-truth hook about a tool the viewer already uses (a stat or 'everyone is doing X wrong') + proof on screen in the first 2 s + a free fix they can apply in 30 s
hook · instagram · confidence medium · seen 1× · 3 posts · avg outlier 7.88×

**How to apply:** Line 1: '[TOOL] [does surprising thing] [N]% of the time - even when [you're wrong / it looks fine].' or 'Everyone's [paying for / overcomplicating] [X]; all you need is [simple thing].' Frame 1: your face + a 6-9 word title box '[How to force TOOL to STOP X]'; by 1-2 s pop the receipt (study screenshot, error, setting). Then 'Here's how to fix it in 30 seconds:' -> exact click path / prompt -> one bonus trick -> 'comment [WORD] for the full prompt'. Use for AI Explained episodes about ChatGPT/Claude behaviour (sycophancy, hallucination, context rot, model choice). Avoid: same topic with a bare caption and no claim (mavgpt's twin post did 4.2K vs 386K).

### 5. Explain the confusing thing beginners literally see in the product (model picker, plugin/skill/MCP menu, a named error) with one analogy ladder and one everyday example per option
topic · instagram · confidence medium · seen 1× · 3 posts · avg outlier 5.1×

**How to apply:** Open on the UI element itself (screenshot of the dropdown/menu) + 'X, Y and Z - what's the difference and when do you use each?'. Then ONE analogy ladder ([X] = middle schooler ... [Z] = PhD) as a simple rating graphic, then one concrete everyday task per option ('Is this French correct? -> Haiku'), then what YOU use and one insider tip (e.g. lower effort to save tokens). 60-90 s, an overlay every ~5 s, jargon named once with a plain definition (nima: 'regression = a new change breaks something that worked'). Ideal AI Explained episode shape for a mixed beginner/engineer audience.
