# Research — ig-build-in-public

2026-10-06 · instagram · build-in-public · 5 insights · 15 outlier posts

## Summary
Across 49 recent reels from 5 builder/developer creators, the reels that beat their creator's normal views by 2.5–150× all **show a finished, working thing in the first frame** (#1), and most build it **for the creator's own life**, with a personal number in the result (#2). Talking-head opinion and career takes with nothing on screen sit at 0.17–0.4× (Danial's AWS/career takes, Riley's sponsored "vibe coding problem").
**Next for Curio on IG:** (1) a 20–40 s reveal that opens on Curio running on the phone with "I built an app that [does Y with what I read/learned]" and the owner's own data plus one real number (#1+#2); (2) a 6–12 s silent loop of one Curio feature working, with "Would you use this?" on screen (#3), as the second post of the day; (3) on the next big model release, "I rebuilt [Curio feature] with [NEW MODEL] in [N hours]" (#4/#5, test only). Keep "Day N" as a small badge, never the hook. Post the first reveal of a feature as the strongest cut, because repeat posts of the same build drop to about 0.5×.
Caveats: views are logged-out grid counters (K/M rounded) and posts under 7 days old are still growing; @sicknider.raw (age-restricted logged-out) and @buildwithcody (profile unavailable) have no data; the 13.2M @pikacodes reel is a Meta collab and is not used as evidence. The outlier table below also includes the parallel AI-tools analyst's creators.

## Top insights

### 1. The finished, working thing is in frame 1 with a one-line 'I made/built [X] that [does Y]' on screen — the build process comes after (or never)
visual · instagram · confidence high · seen 1× · 5 posts · avg outlier 5.64×

**How to apply:** First frame = the result running/in your hand (app on the phone, dashboard live, gadget working), text overlay ≤10 words: 'I built [THING] that [SURPRISING JOB]' or 'Would you use this [THING] I made? It [DOES Y]'. Then (optional) how: problem → steps → result again. Use for every Curio feature reveal. Avoid: opening on your face explaining, logos, or 'Day N' alone. Follow-ups of the same build decay (0.44-0.51x) — post the first reveal as the strongest cut.

### 3. ≤15 s silent or near-silent single-shot loop with one text line (question or year/role label) — rewatch bait that beat 30-60 s talking heads by 5-150x

**How to apply:** Make 1 in 3 reels a 6-12 s clip: one take, no voiceover, music/ambient, one line of text on screen the whole time.
