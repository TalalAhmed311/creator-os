# Research — x-build-in-public

2026-10-06 · x · build-in-public · 5 insights · 15 outlier posts

## Summary
Data: 67 recent original posts (no replies/reposts, all ≥ 24 h old) from 6 tracked X builders, counters from the public X API (verified). Make Curio's next posts **show the app working**: a 15–60 s screen recording of one Curio feature with a 1–3 line outcome caption (#1, ~8× creator median on 4 creators, incl. the 10–13K accounts samuelrdt and orcdev).
Turn every small number into a **result + cause + takeaway** post with a simple before/after chart ("0 → N waitlist signups. No ads. Just [X].") — plain milestones sit at 0.6–0.7× (#2).
Fill the 1–3/day cadence with **one-line contrarian takes** from building Curio and **image-led posts** (screenshot/before-after, ≤ 12 words) — cheap to make, reply/bookmark magnets (#3, #4).
Never put the curioapp.online link or an ask in the body; link in a self-reply (#5). Caveat: these are 10K–400K accounts; for a new account, replies under these creators' posts are the main distribution until posts have proof to show.

## Top insights

### 1. Show the thing working: a 15-120 s screen-recording/demo video with a 1-3 line outcome caption is the biggest reach lever for product posts
format · x · confidence high · seen 1× · 4 posts · avg outlier 7.92×

**How to apply:** Template: '[What it does, as an outcome or a what-if].\n\n[One line: how it works / built with what].\n\n[Who you built it with or what inspired it].' + attach a 15-60 s screen recording of [Curio doing the thing] - first frame already mid-action, no logo intro. Use whenever a feature visibly works (new screen, AI answer, mobile build running). Avoid: posting the same demo again as an 'update' the next day (1.05x), and caption-only reactions to your own video without a contrast or number (0.47x).

### 2. Keep links and asks out of the post body: winners give first and put 'try it' / repo links in a self-reply; posts with a URL or upvote/LIVE ask in the body sit at 0.3-0.8x
cta · x · confidence medium · seen 1× · 3 posts · avg outlier 8.45×

**How to apply:** Post body = hook + proof (video/image) + takeaway, no URL. Then reply to yourself: 'try it here: [curioapp.online]' (alexcooldev's link replies still got 1-4K views). Asks ('give us a star', 'join the waitlist') go after the value, without a URL in the body. Avoid: 'LIVE: … [link]' (0.34x), 'Upvotes appreciated [PH link]' (0.78x), '[demo] on [yourproduct.com]' (0.47x).

hook · x · confidence medium · seen 1× · 3 posts · avg outlier 6.04×

**How to apply:** Template: '[Thing you did] [when].\n\n[Surprising result with an exact small number].\n\nNo [usual effort 1].\nNo [usual effort 2].\nNo [usual effort 3].\n\nJust [the actual cause].\n\n[One-line takeaway other builders can steal].' Avoid plain milestones with no cause or lesson ('crossed 150 stars!' = 0.73x, '10,000 downloads' = 0.61x).
